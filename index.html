A GAME BY LUNAPHASE GAMES

Out of Line

A multiplayer social deduction game about a shared canvas, mismatched prompts, and friends trying to work out who got the odd one.

Paint together. Compare the result. Figure out who has a different prompt.

THE GAME

Everyone's painting.
Not everyone has the same prompt.

In Out of Line, players use paintball-style tools to build one messy picture on a shared wall. Most people have one prompt. Others receive a different one — and may not realise it.

Look at the canvas, talk through the clues, and decide whose contribution doesn't quite make sense. Then cast your vote before the round moves on.

HOW A ROUND WORKS

Make a mess.
Read between the splatters.

01

Get your prompt

Players receive a word to paint. Not every prompt matches.

02

Paint the same wall

Add colour to the shared canvas with paintball-style tools. The group picture is the clue.

03

Talk, then vote

Compare what you see, defend your choices, and vote for the player who seems out of line.

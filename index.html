01THE IDEA

One picture.
More than one story.

Out of Line is a multiplayer social deduction game built around a shared canvas. Everyone gets the same prompt — but one player doesn't quite have the same information.

Draw your part, study everyone else's, then work out who's improvising. It helps to know your friends. It doesn't guarantee anything.

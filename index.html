THE IDEA

One prompt.
Different stories.

Out of Line is a social deduction game built around a shared drawing. Most players know what they're trying to make. One player has to work it out without giving themselves away.

Add a few friends, take turns on the canvas, then talk through what looks right — and what doesn't. Being a good artist isn't the point. Convincing everyone is another matter.

HOW A ROUND GOES

It's simple until
someone gets nervous.

01

Make your part

Take your turn and add to the group's picture. Everyone's choices become part of the evidence.

02

Look at the whole thing

Compare the details, ask questions, and figure out who's making it up as they go.

03

Make your case

Talk it out and vote. A confident explanation can help — unless everyone has noticed the same thing.

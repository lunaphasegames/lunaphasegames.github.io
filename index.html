SO, WHAT'S THE IDEA?

A party game about being almost right.

One wall.
Many bad ideas.
One very odd painter.

Out of Line is a multiplayer social deduction game where everyone adds paint to one shared canvas. Most players get the same word. Someone may get a different one.

Look at the picture taking shape, swap theories, and work out who isn't quite painting the same idea. Artistic talent isn't the point. Explaining why you painted that giant purple blob? That's part of the fun.
